Augmented Analytics in SAP Analytics Cloud
A Study of Impact and User Adoption
Overview

This repository contains my research paper on Augmented Analytics in SAP Analytics Cloud (SAC).
The study explores how AI-powered analytics features such as Smart Discovery, Smart Insights, Smart Predict, and Natural Language Querying impact business decision-making, efficiency, and user adoption.

The research also examines the challenges of trust, explainability, and data governance in AI-assisted analytics systems.

Abstract

Traditional Business Intelligence systems are evolving into AI-driven analytics ecosystems.
This research evaluates the effectiveness and adoption of Augmented Analytics in SAP Analytics Cloud by analyzing:

Time-to-insight improvements
AI-generated business insights
User trust in automated analytics
Adoption factors influencing enterprise users
Predictive analytics capabilities in SAC

The study uses a mixed-methods approach involving comparative performance analysis and structured surveys conducted with SAP professionals.
